Our Story

Started in 2012 with a simple mission

In 2012 I set off on a mission to bring specialty coffee from my village to the world. The area of Kanungu, on the western border of Uganda, and its surrounding districts have been producing coffee for generations.

Historically, all of the coffee had been sold to middlemen for $0.03 – $0.05 per pound. Because of its low quality it was labelled “non-exportable: local consumption only”. I set out to change that.

In partnership with groups like Temple Coffee Roasters (Sacramento, CA), we began training local farmers in proper growing and harvesting techniques. Over time this work produced drastic increases in cherry quality. We built a facility to process raw cherries into green coffee beans.

Improving quality (and therefore the price point) goes far beyond the coffee itself. Along the way we have addressed education, agricultural practices, social dynamics, quality of living and infrastructure for local farmers. This allows us to pay living wages and support community projects such as clean well water and higher education — including schools and an accredited university.

Gorilla Summit Coffee is a social enterprise working with smallholder farmers to bring more money into communities.

On Location

From farm to table across the southwest highlands

Kanungu

Main production area

Our primary group of farmers. Coffee is farmed and harvested in these hills near the borders of Rwanda, Congo and Uganda — a stone’s throw from Queen Elizabeth National Park.

Photo: Kisoro

Kisoro

Second largest group

Known for Lake Mutanda, fertile volcanic soil and the iconic Mount Sabinyo. Our second main farming location.

Bwindi

Volcanic soils & Batwa communities

Deep, dark volcanic soil and access to another important group of farmers. Home to the short Batwa people and the famous Bwindi Impenetrable Forest.

Kampala

Headquarters

Our Coffee & Services

Quality from cherry to cup

Quality is the cornerstone of Gorilla Summit Coffee. Quality extends beyond the coffee into the environment where it grows and the people it touches. By producing only the highest quality 100% Arabica as well as Robusta, we are able to pay a higher price to the coffee farmers and invest profits in community development projects.

We supply specialty coffee to premier roasters and distributors around the world, with most of the coffee going to the United States, Europe and the Middle East.

Harvesting & Processing

Training in proper growing and harvesting techniques. Facility for turning raw cherries into green coffee beans. Natural and honey processes refined for both flavour and lower emissions.

Roasting

Careful roasting that respects the unique character of each highland lot — clean, complex profiles with the potential for high cupping scores.

Packaging

Professional packaging ready for export and specialty markets, protecting quality from origin to the roaster or café.

Farmer Support

Seedlings, training, and ongoing technical support so farmers can produce the best quality specialty coffee in Uganda and the Great Lakes region.

Impact & Community

Coffee that lifts communities and protects the land

Gorilla Summit Coffee is a social enterprise. Every bag of specialty coffee supports better prices for farmers and real community projects in the highlands of southwest Uganda.

Living Wages

We pay significantly higher prices than traditional middlemen. This allows farming families to earn a living wage and invest in their children’s future.

Clean Water

Premiums from specialty coffee help fund clean well water projects so communities have safe drinking water close to home.

Education

Support for schools and higher education — including an accredited university — so the next generation has more opportunities beyond the farm.

Carbon & Soil

We are moving toward carbon-neutral production, water conservation, and organic transition using living organisms to restore soil health.
