Our Story

Rooted in the highlands of Uganda

Gorilla Summit Coffee is born from the rich volcanic soils and cool mountain air of Uganda’s highlands. We work directly with smallholder farmers who grow exceptional Arabica at elevations that produce complex, clean, and vibrant flavors.

Every lot is carefully selected, processed, and roasted with respect for the land, the farmers, and the people who drink it. We believe great coffee should be transparent, ethical, and memorable.
