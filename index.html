Our Story

Started with a simple mission

In 2012 we set out to bring specialty coffee from the highlands of southwest Uganda to the world. The area of Kanungu and its surrounding districts have been producing coffee for generations, yet historically the coffee was sold to middlemen at extremely low prices and labelled for local consumption only.

In partnership with groups like Temple Coffee Roasters (Sacramento, CA), we began training local farmers in proper growing and harvesting techniques. Quality improved dramatically. We built a processing facility and started exporting specialty-grade coffee.

Today Gorilla Summit Coffee works with over 3,800 smallholder farmers. Improving quality and price goes far beyond the coffee itself — it supports education, better agricultural practices, and stronger communities in the shadow of the mountain gorillas.

Impact & Community

Coffee that supports people and place

Gorilla Summit Coffee is a social enterprise. Every bag supports better prices for farmers and real community projects in the highlands of southwest Uganda. We align our work with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

Better prices for farmers

We pay significantly above the local market rate so families can invest in education, health and their farms.

Quality training

Hands-on training in selective harvesting, processing and quality control turns ordinary coffee into specialty coffee.

Community projects

Education support, health initiatives and infrastructure improvements that benefit entire villages.

Conservation link

Working in the buffer zones of Bwindi and the Virungas, we help show that thriving coffee communities can coexist with mountain gorilla habitat.

Our Locations

From farm to table across the southwest highlands

Kanungu Highlands

Kanungu

Main production heartland

Our primary farming communities sit in the rolling hills of Kanungu District, near the borders of Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of Congo. This is where most of our cherries are grown and where our processing facility is based.

Kisoro & Lake Mutanda

Kisoro

Volcanic soils · Second largest group

Known for Lake Mutanda and the dramatic peaks of the Virunga range. Rich volcanic soil produces distinctive cup profiles. A gateway to gorilla trekking in Mgahinga and Bwindi.

Bwindi Forest Edge

Bwindi

Gorilla habitat · Batwa communities

On the edge of Bwindi Impenetrable Forest — home to half of the world’s remaining mountain gorillas. High altitude and volcanic soils create excellent conditions for specialty coffee.

Kampala HQ

Kampala

Headquarters · Logistics hub

Our main offices coordinate exports, quality control and relationships with international buyers while staying connected to the highland farming communities.
