From Farm to Export

The real journey of Gorilla Summit Coffee

1. Harvesting
Farmers carefully pick ripe cherries by hand on family farms in the highlands.

2. On the Farm
Working side-by-side with smallholder farmers to improve quality and yields.

3. Drying
Cherries are carefully dried on raised beds under the highland sun — a critical step for specialty quality.

4. Packaging
Specialty coffee is packed in branded jute bags ready for export.

5. Ready for Export
Sacks prepared and staged for international buyers.

6. Community
Better prices and projects that support families and livelihoods around the farms.

Quality from cherry to cup

Quality is the cornerstone of Gorilla Summit Coffee. By producing only the highest quality Arabica and Robusta, we are able to pay a higher price to the coffee farmers and invest profits in community development projects.

We supply specialty coffee to premier roasters and distributors around the world, with most of the coffee going to the United States, Europe and the Middle East.
